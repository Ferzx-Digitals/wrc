# Interview Notes - World Ranger Congress Website

**Date:** 2026-02-14
**Source:** spec.pdf (76 pages) + 6 wireframe PDFs

---

## Feature Summary & Goals

Build a complete website for the **11th IRF World Ranger Congress** to be held in **Puerto Iguazu, Misiones, Argentina** from **19-23 April 2027**. The site replaces/updates the previous 10th WRC website (France 2024). It is a multi-page informational and registration website for 600+ rangers, conservation professionals, and environmental practitioners worldwide.

**Theme:** "Recognising Rangers: Advancing the profession to safeguard our future"

**Organizers:** International Ranger Federation (IRF) + Syndicate of National Rangers of Argentina Republic (SIGUNARA)

---

## Navigation Structure

Main nav items: **Home | About (dropdown) | Submit | FAQ | Congress Theme | Plan Travel (dropdown) | Program | Contact Us | Press | Register Now**

- About dropdown: Congress Introduction, Congress Events
- Plan Travel dropdown: Logistics, Venue and Accommodation, Travel FAQs

### 6. PLAN TRAVEL PAGE (3 sub-sections)

**Sub-tab 1: Logistics**
- **Visa details:** Argentina visa requirements, Argentine visa portal link, check eligibility link
- **How to reach Puerto Iguazu:**
  - By Air: Cataratas del Iguazu International Airport (IGR), 20km from town. From Buenos Aires ~1h50m.

- **Booking:** Calendar widget, book accommodation dates 18-24 April 2027
- **What accommodation includes:** Room, breakfast, dinner, evening functions
- **NOTE:** Need to add 2 other available hotels as well

**Sub-tab 3: Travel FAQs**
- Local currency: Argentine Peso (ARS). USD and Brazilian reais also accepted in tourist areas.
- WiFi/telephone: Local SIM cards (Claro, Movistar, Personal). Hotels have WiFi.
- Average temperature April: Mild/warm, 26-28°C highs, 17-19°C lows
- Rainfall April: 150-200mm, 10-12 rainy days, afternoon thunderstorms
- Cloudiness: 40-50% coverage
- Sunrise/sunset: ~6:00-6:20 AM / ~6:00-6:10 PM
- Humidity: 70-75% average
- Wind: 10-15 km/h, mostly calm
- What to pack: Light clothing, light jacket, comfortable shoes, rain gear

## Design Philosophy (from wireframes)

### Visual Elements:
- **Jungle silhouette** graphics at bottom of sections
- **Easter eggs** throughout: Iguazu terrain, flora & fauna of the region (toucans, jaguars, butterflies, etc.)
- **Waterfall wave/splash** graphic dividers
- **World map** vector/animation for regions section
- **Rotating panel billboard** style photo columns (like flip panels)
- **Countdown clock** with animated design
- **Google Maps** integration (stylized, no boundaries)

### Interaction Patterns:
- Carousel/slider sections throughout
- Hover-to-expand cards (objectives, activities)
- Tab navigation within pages (Program: Overview/Schedule/Activities/Field Trips)
- Accordion FAQ sections with search
- Stacked bottom bars that open as tabs when clicked
- 5-card setup with hover pick-up interaction

### Color/Style:
- Nature-themed (greens, earth tones, blues)
- Mountain/forest silhouette borders
- Professional yet adventurous feel
- High-resolution nature photography
- IRF copyright logo must be used

## Technical Decisions & Constraints

- Static/CMS website (content-driven, not application-heavy)
- Multi-language consideration (content in English, forms in EN/ES/FR)
- Registration handled via external forms (Google Forms) or integrated registration system
- Payment: Mercado Pago (Argentina) + PayPal (International)
- Google Maps embeds for venue/location
- Responsive design required (international audience)
- Heavy imagery (nature photography, ranger photos, logos)
- Countdown timer (JavaScript)
- PDF downloads for program/agenda/press kit
